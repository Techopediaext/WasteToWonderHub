What is happening?

Bandwidth is the maximum amount of data that a network connection can transfer in a given amount of time. When network demand stays below the available bandwidth, data can move efficiently. When demand becomes greater than the available bandwidth, congestion occurs and data begins moving more slowly. Connection quality also affects performance. Old or damaged cables, devices that are too far from the router, and nearby interference can reduce connection quality and slow data transfer.
